KIGALI HOMETECH STORE - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Version 1.0 | Last updated: September 2026

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept cash at the store, mobile money, bank cards, and approved bank transfers.

Q: Can I return a product?
A: Eligible products may be returned within 30 calendar days with proof of purchase. See the Return Policy for full conditions.

Q: How long does delivery take in Kigali?
A: In-stock items normally arrive within 1 to 2 business days after order confirmation.

Q: Do you deliver outside Kigali?
A: Contact the store for a quote. Some outside-Kigali deliveries may be arranged.

Q: How do I claim a warranty?
A: Bring the product and proof of purchase to the service desk.

Q: Where can I find product instructions?
A: Check the relevant product manual or ask the store support team.
